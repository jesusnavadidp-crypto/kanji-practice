# RTK keywords

`rtk.js` contains the 2,200 numbered kanji in *Remembering the Kanji 1*, sixth edition. Entries are keyed by character, with `number` and `keyword` fields. Kanji outside this volume have no RTK entry; radical variants are not automatically assigned the keyword of another character.

Source: https://github.com/cyphar/heisig-rtk-index/blob/master/kanji/KANJI_INDEX.csv
Retrieved 2026-10-18. Extracted only `kanji`, `id_6th_ed`, and `keyword_6th_ed`, filtering frame numbers 1–2200. The source also contains later-volume entries, which are excluded. The upstream project supplies a CC0 dedication (included as `RTK-CC0.txt`). These mappings follow that community index; they have not been independently proofread against every printed frame.

Data is committed as a plain script, with no network request or build step required. Existing dictionary meanings remain separate.
